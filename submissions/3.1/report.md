## Design outcome

White and ballet-pink composition with custom optical wordmark treatment, credible satin bow and distinct latex balloon. Laptop1440×900 and1280×800 and independently reflowed mobile390×844 and360×800 were inspected. Entry is immediate: Find an exercise or Choose an exercise. Five optional original questions each have matching SVG geometry, question-specific labels and equal large controls. No account, typing, demographic disclosure, response deadline or audio is required.

The routing table was defined before recommendation implementation. Current choice and refusal take priority. Explicit comfort can justify a suggestion; skipped preferences remain unknown. Q1 versus max(Q2,Q3) is compared privately only with complete cues and both activities comfortable. Ties/missing information produce equal choices. No total anxiety score, red-face count, click-speed inference or crisis classification exists. The transparent ready screen gives a short scripted reason, Start and an alternative.

Breathing is a small timing experiment:4s inhale/6s exhale, six cycles, no hold. One monotonic clock drives the cues and continuous balloon deformation. It is a timing metaphor, not measurement. Preparation/instruction/transition/ending time is included in the planned78s budget; grounding has a90s assumed budget and five private notice-and-Next screens (5seen/4felt/3heard/2smelled/1tasted). The separate check-in assumption is~28s. These are design assumptions, not measured human performance or forced deadlines; chosen pauses/support time is excluded under the supplied assignment rule.

The future model has one small validated explanation request at the recommendation boundary, grounded in actual structured turns and approved rule output, with canned fallback. It cannot diagnose, score, choose a skill, override refusal, change instructions/timing, or decide support access. Future session/turn history, answer/skip versions, reason codes, timestamps/status, explicit visual preferences and generated explanation provenance are planned distinctly. No live model/backend/database/persistence is implemented. The two history records and failure state are visibly fictional/scripted.
